# DNB Customer Hackathon - Lakebase

## Event Information

**Date & Time:** Wednesday, January 28, 2026, 9:00 AM–2:45 PM PST
**Format:** Virtual
**Platform:** Databricks-provided Lighthouse environment (free trial accounts)

## About

This hackathon is organized by Databricks and DNB, focused on exploring use cases with Lakebase. Teams will compete in two separate judging tracks based on their chosen use case category.

## Prizes

Each use case category has separate prizes:
- **1st Place:** $250 gift card (team share)
- **2nd Place:** $150 gift card (team share)
- **3rd Place:** $100 gift card (team share)

## Judging Criteria

Submissions will be evaluated on:
- **Insights Quality** - The depth and value of insights generated
- **Design** - Technical architecture and implementation quality
- **Storytelling** - How effectively the solution is presented and communicated
- **Innovation** - Creative approaches and novel applications

## Requirements

To participate, you must:
1. Review and agree to the [Official Rules](https://events.databricks.com/FY260128-SD-DNB-Named-Lakebase-Hackathon/registration)
2. Accept Databricks' Master Cloud Services Agreement and Privacy Notice
3. Complete the Team Formation Form (link available on registration page)
4. Register with required information (name, email, job title, company, phone, country)
